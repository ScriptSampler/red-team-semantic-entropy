\paragraph{Setup.}
The victim model is Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct in 4-bit quantisation on a single 16\,GB GPU.
Semantic entropy draws $N{=}10$ answers at temperature $1.0$ and clusters them by
bidirectional DeBERTa-large-MNLI entailment; semantic reformulation entropy uses
$R{=}3$ reformulations, $K{=}8$, temperature $0.8$. Our SE replication on TriviaQA
reaches AUROC $0.787$ (published SE figure $0.828$; the gap is the NLI backend, see
Limitations). Targets are drawn by the stratified score-independent sampler; on the
resulting fair pool the clean detector scores AUROC $0.704$ [$0.653$, $0.753$], against
the $1.0$ that score-dependent selection manufactures by construction.

\paragraph{Reading the null control.}
Table~\ref{tab:nullcontrol} places the optimised attack against two null bands---a
same-question \emph{seed-noise} floor and a \emph{benign-paraphrase} floor of $m$
NLI-passing non-adversarial rephrasings, with $m$ matched to the attacker's
$\sim$181-candidate search budget---under each of four clustering relations. ($m$ is
distinct from SRE's samples-per-reformulation $K$.) The
shared-NLI arm is the detector's own clusterer and is the \emph{confounded upper bound}:
it cannot separate a real change in the answer distribution from the NLI model's own
inconsistency, because that same model gates the attack's paraphrases. Exact-match is a
\emph{strict lower bound} that saturates on short factoid answers. The
\texttt{e5-base-unsupervised} embedding relation is NLI-supervision-free but near-chance
on the adversarial, word-preserving case (hard-negative AUROC $0.51$), so it cannot
adjudicate. The LLM-judge (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), victim- and NLI-independent and
validated to $0.93$ [$0.90$, $0.96$] ($n{=}300$) on domain-matched gold-alias hard
negatives---a proxy for the deployed clustering decision---is the \textbf{adjudicator}
(for the false-alarm direction, under the disclosed re-scoped gate; Methods). The
\textbf{headline} is the per-target \emph{paired} comparison of attack-max to a
\emph{budget-matched} benign-max (the max over a benign search of the attacker's own size),
which is the only winner's-curse-honest statistic; the percentile-within-individual-draws
and the net-vs-mean are shown as diagnostics but are budget-biased upward (a max over the
attacker's $\sim$180 candidates beats individual benign draws by construction) and are not
the claim statistic.

\paragraph{Decision rule (single, pre-registered, with disclosed deviations).}
Reframe~(a)---``the attack targets the detector''---is claimed iff, at $n{\geq}80$, the
adjudicator clusterer's per-target \emph{paired} net (attack-max minus the
\emph{budget-matched} benign-max, i.e.\ the max over $\sim$180 random feasible paraphrases,
matching the attacker's search budget) has a paired-bootstrap $95\%$ CI strictly above
zero, corroborated by the sign test (fraction of targets with attack-max $>$ benign-max)
and read against the analytic IID null baseline ($\approx m/(m{+}1)$ for a max over $m$
candidates). The adjudicator is the validated LLM-judge. If the CI includes zero, (a) is
not claimed and the NLI/exact-match bracket plus the judge point estimate is reported as
the honest bounded result. We retire the earlier percentile-within-individual-draws and
survival-ratio statistics as headlines: both are winner's-curse-biased, since the attack
move is a max over $\sim$180 candidates while the individual benign draws are not (a
max-of-180 sits at $\sim$$99\%$ of an individual-draw distribution under $H_0$, not
$50\%$). Two deviations from the original pre-registration are disclosed as
outcome-triggered: the adjudicator moved from the sentence embedder to the LLM-judge
(triggered by the embedder's $0.51$ hard-negative AUROC), and the benign floor moved from
individual draws to the budget-matched max (triggered by the winner's-curse budget mismatch
surfaced in adversarial review).
