\paragraph{Setup.}
The victim model is Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct in 4-bit quantisation on a single 16\,GB GPU.
Semantic entropy draws $N{=}10$ answers at temperature $1.0$ and clusters them by
bidirectional DeBERTa-large-MNLI entailment; semantic reformulation entropy uses
$R{=}3$ reformulations, $K{=}8$, temperature $0.8$. Our SE replication on TriviaQA
reaches AUROC $0.787$ (published SE figure $0.828$; the gap is the NLI backend, see
Limitations). Targets are drawn by the stratified score-independent sampler; on the
resulting fair pool the clean detector scores AUROC $0.69$, against the $1.0$ that
score-dependent selection manufactures by construction.

\paragraph{Reading the null control.}
Table~\ref{tab:nullcontrol} places the optimised attack against two null bands---a
same-question \emph{seed-noise} floor and a \emph{benign-paraphrase} floor of $K$
NLI-passing non-adversarial rephrasings---under each of four clustering relations. The
shared-NLI arm is the detector's own clusterer and is the \emph{confounded upper bound}:
it cannot separate a real change in the answer distribution from the NLI model's own
inconsistency, because that same model gates the attack's paraphrases. Exact-match is a
\emph{strict lower bound} that saturates on short factoid answers. The
\texttt{e5-base-unsupervised} embedding relation is NLI-supervision-free but near-chance
on the adversarial, word-preserving case (hard-negative AUROC $0.51$), so it cannot
adjudicate. The LLM-judge (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), victim- and NLI-independent and
validated to $0.92$ on exactly that discrimination, is the \textbf{adjudicator}. The
\textbf{headline} is the attack's \emph{percentile within the benign-move distribution}
(null-controlled and scale-free); the net move (attack $-$ mean benign) and the
pre-registered survival ratio (judge-net~$/$~NLI-net) are supplementary, since the net is
inflated wherever an arm's benign floor is low.
